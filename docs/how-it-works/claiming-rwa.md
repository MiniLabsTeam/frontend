# Claiming Physical Cars

## Eligibility Requirements

To claim a physical diecast model car:

* Own a **Legendary** rarity car NFT
* Have all **4 spare part slots** equipped
* The car must **not** have been claimed before

## Claim Steps

### 1. Check Eligibility
Go to **Claim** page. Eligible cars are displayed with a **Claim** button.

### 2. Enter Shipping Info
Provide your shipping details:
* Full name
* Phone number
* Complete shipping address (street, city, country, zip)

### 3. Confirm Claim
* An on-chain transaction records the claim
* Your car NFT is marked as "claimed"
* The car is removed from marketplace trading

### 4. Track Delivery
Monitor your claim status:

| Status | What's Happening |
|--------|-----------------|
| Pending | Claim submitted, awaiting review |
| Processing | Order confirmed, being prepared |
| Shipped | Package sent, tracking number available |
| Delivered | Package received |

## Important Notes

* **One claim per car** — each legendary car can only be claimed once
* **NFT stays in wallet** — you still own the digital asset
* **No more trading** — claimed cars are locked from marketplace
* **Shipping times vary** — delivery depends on your location
* **Parts included** — equipped spare parts are represented in the physical model
