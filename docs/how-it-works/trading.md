# Trading on Marketplace

## Selling an NFT

1. Go to **Inventory** page
2. Select the car or spare part you want to sell
3. Tap **List for Sale**
4. Set your price in OCT
5. Set expiry duration
6. Confirm the transaction — your NFT is escrowed on-chain

> Your NFT is locked in the smart contract while listed. You can cancel the listing at any time to get it back.

## Buying an NFT

1. Go to **Marketplace** page
2. Browse listings or use filters:
   * **Type** — Cars or Spare Parts
   * **Rarity** — Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary
   * **Brand** — Lamborghini, Ferrari, Ford, Chevrolet
   * **Price Range** — Min/max OCT
3. Tap on a listing to view full details and stats
4. Tap **Buy** and confirm the OCT payment
5. The NFT is transferred to your wallet instantly

## Cancelling a Listing

1. Go to **Inventory** → **My Listings**
2. Find the active listing
3. Tap **Cancel**
4. Confirm the transaction — your NFT is returned

## Fee Breakdown

When a sale occurs:

```
Buyer pays: Listed Price
Seller receives: Listed Price - Marketplace Fee - Royalty Fee

Marketplace Fee: 2.5% → Platform Treasury
Royalty Fee: 2.5% → Original Creator
Seller receives: 95% of listed price
```

## Trading Tips

* **Check stats carefully** — a Rare car with great stats can outperform a poorly-rolled Epic
* **Brand matters for parts** — spare parts only fit their compatible brand
* **Watch the market** — prices fluctuate based on supply and demand
* **List at competitive prices** — check similar listings before setting your price
